What's Inside

Two Hands. Infinite Possibilities.

Dual Grabpack Hands

Blue left, red right. Each hand fires independently — grab objects, activate scanners, or latch onto swing handles.

Rope Physics

10 metres of cable that wraps realistically around pillars, walls, and obstacles. When it's taut, it's taut — no cheating the limit.

Pendulum Swing

Latch onto overhead handles and swing across gaps with real pendulum momentum. Pump with WASD, release to launch.

Puzzle Doors

Color-coded scanner panels require the matching hand. Aim carefully — wrong hand gets rejected. Right hand opens the way.
